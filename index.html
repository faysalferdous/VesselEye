Scope, data quality & privacy

Operational boundaries and source transparency

Independent prototype

Designed for a Bangladesh maritime research presentation. No official Navy insignia, endorsement, access or affiliation is claimed.

Data provenance

Historical records are dated snapshots; Wikidata results are community-maintained and may be outdated. The map uses fictional markers only. Do not use for navigation.

Privacy & security

No military vessel monitoring, crew identities, personal contact data, internal systems or live AIS. No credentials or paid APIs are required. Search terms go to the public cloud API; saved records remain in your browser.
